              TiMer

              Every Second, Perfectly.

              A modern time-management application designed to make everyday time tracking simple, precise, and efficient.

              Core features

              • Precision stopwatch — accurate to the millisecond, with lap recording and session statistics.
              • Voice-controlled countdown — hands-free timer control with spoken feedback where supported.
              • Multiple independent timers — run as many countdowns as you need, side by side.
              • Preset library — one-tap timers for work, study, breaks, cooking, and everyday routines.
              • History & insights — a local record of completed timers and stopwatch sessions.
              • Offline-ready PWA — installable and fully usable without a connection.
